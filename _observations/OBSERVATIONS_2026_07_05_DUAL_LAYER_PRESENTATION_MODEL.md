The Dual-Layer Presentation Model, Provenance Toggle & Set-Piece Triage

Date: 2026-07-05. Status: governing principle for all member-facing surfaces, founder-ratified. Type: append-only. This records and names patterns the frozen architecture already implies; it introduces no new source of truth, no new data path, and no change to the layer structure or governance model.

1. The dual-layer model

Every member-facing surface has two layers: the fact layer (the authoritative card/data view, derived directly from the immutable ledger) and, where warranted, the illustrated layer (a rendered room that is a pure visual presentation of the fact layer). The illustrated layer is a pure function of the fact layer — a room shows a trophy because the resolver says the member holds it, never because a trophy was placed in the render; if the data says empty, the room shows empty; where the two could disagree, the fact layer wins. The art is a lens, not a ledger. (This is 1.7a's renderer rule — clothe structure, never alter content — applied to rooms.)

2. The provenance toggle

Every illustrated room carries a visible provenance toggle, available to all viewers. Default is the illustrated room; toggling reveals, for each displayed element, its receipt — the existing two-tier provenance treatment (Source Facts Verified / ATTESTED · Not Canonical), fact ID, custody / mark-movement history where applicable, entered-into-the-record. The card/data fact layer is the provenance the toggle reveals. It exists as a demonstration of the values: it lets anyone verify the beauty hides nothing — every object traces to an immutable, verified fact. Not hidden, not developer-only; it is how the product shows its work.

3. Set-piece triage — not every surface earns a room

Rooms are reserved for emotional scoring chances, as set pieces are in football: the choreography is saved for the dangerous areas, not spent on every throw-in. Surfaces classify into three kinds:

Set-piece surfaces (full illustrated room + provenance toggle): surfaces about memory, pride, belonging, and the league's affectionate ribbing — the Clubhouse, the Coach Office, the Trophy Hall, the Hall of Fame & Shame. Maximum craft.
Administrative surfaces (clean, functional, no room, by design): consent, sign-in, admin panels, the raw data/provenance view itself. Dressing these up would harm them; the provenance toggle already keeps the data reachable everywhere, so they need no room. Quick and correct is the goal.
Hybrid surfaces (functional with an emotional accent): the members directory, the archive — mostly utilitarian with a touch of warmth where it lands.
Restraint everywhere except where impact is earned. A product that painted every screen would exhaust; one that paints the right screens is a cathedral with sensible hallways.

4. Nostalgic accretion — how rooms live over time

Set-piece rooms are designed to accrue member-contributed nostalgia over time and through onboarding — photos, team logos, board notes, remembered moments, oral history, the "Forget it!!!" moments the members themselves nominate. A room that is identical in year five to year one is a painting; a room that fills with the members' own history is a home. This accretion is the emotional payload — and it is how a room is "alive": not through motion (ruled off-constitution as an engagement loop), but through the accumulation of real member memory. All such content is member-contributed, consent-gated (CO-R1), append-only, and never fabricated — nostalgia is collected and displayed, never invented (silence over speculation, applied to memory).

5. Constitutional standing

These patterns are the core architecture made visible (facts immutable and append-only; narratives derived never fact-creating; silence over speculation — an element with no fact shows an honest empty state, never a fabricated object; no engagement loop — the toggle reveals truth on demand, accretion adds real memory, neither optimizes nor holds attention). No architectural change; the frozen design, expressed and made visible.

6. Scope and sequence

Governs all member-facing surfaces going forward. The four-axis Dual-Layer Room Map in Completion Plan v1.4 inventories every data view against { data-layer status · surface class · room status · nostalgic payload } and sequences the build. Build order honors the calendar: the set-piece rooms that make the Draft-Weekend reveal land (Clubhouse, Coach Office, Trophy Hall) ship before the season; the Hall of Fame & Shame, the nostalgic-accretion features, and onboarding-collected memory are richer with time and members and sequence mid-season and beyond. The provenance toggle builds first in the Trophy Hall, then retrofits to the other rooms.
